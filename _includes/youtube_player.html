{% comment %} Usage ===== {% include youtube_player.html id='A8a5Yj9ugQA' %} Shows the video's thumbnail with a play button. js/youtube-player.js swaps in YouTube's player when it's clicked, so the player's 1 MB of scripts loads only for someone watching. Without JavaScript the thumbnail links to the video on YouTube. The thumbnail is the 640px one (sddefault). Some older videos don't have it, and YouTube then sends a 120x90 placeholder instead of an error, so onload switches those to the 480px one (hqdefault). It loads straight away at high priority, not lazily: the video is usually near the top of its post and the largest thing on screen. {% endcomment %}
